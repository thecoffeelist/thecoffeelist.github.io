The Coffee List

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Coffee List is an iPhone app where people rate cafés: the coffee, the matcha, the vibe, how good the place is for working, and who goes there. This policy explains what personal data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

What we collect and why

DataWhat it is used for
Email address and passwordCreating your account and signing you in. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. Other users never see your email.
Sign in with AppleIf you use it, Apple gives us an account ID and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address). We use them only to sign you in.
Your display nameShown next to your reviews.
Your reviews and the cafés you addScores, drink, milk, hot or iced, tastes, crowd, price and notes. Reviews are public to all users of the app, with your display name.
Reports and blocksWhen you report a review or block a user, we store that so we can review the report and hide the blocked user's reviews from you.
LocationOnly if you allow it in iOS. Your location is used on your phone to centre the map and sort cafés by distance. When you search for a café, a rounded position (about 1 km accuracy) is sent with the search so nearby cafés come first. We do not store your location.
Café searchesWhat you type when adding a café is sent to Google Places, or to the OpenStreetMap search service Photon (komoot), to find matching cafés.
Technical dataLike any internet service, the servers that answer the app see your IP address and basic request information. Our service providers use it to deliver the service and keep it secure.

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, accounts and sign-in. Your account and reviews are stored there.
  • Google Places API (Google LLC): café search. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Photon by komoot using OpenStreetMap data: café search and map positions.
  • Apple: the map in the app (Apple Maps), Sign in with Apple, app distribution.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU data protection law applies, transfers rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • Your account, reviews, cafés and search: performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Reports, blocks, security and abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in a safe, trustworthy app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Location: your consent, which you give and can withdraw at any time in the iOS settings (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

How long we keep data

We keep your account and reviews until you delete your account. Deleting your account (in the app: Account → Delete account) removes your account, your display name, your reviews, your reports and your blocks. Cafés you added stay on the list without any link to you. Backups held by our providers are overwritten within their normal backup cycle.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time. Write to the email address above for anything else. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident, you have similar rights to know about, correct and delete your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children

The Coffee List is not meant for children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, and at least 16 in the EU unless a parent agrees.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it on this page and change the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app.
