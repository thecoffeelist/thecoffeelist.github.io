The Coffee List

the coffee list

An iPhone app where friends rate cafés worldwide: the coffee, the matcha, the vibe, how laptop-friendly a place is, and who you'll find there.

Help

Add a café

Tap + Add a place, search for the café by name and city, and pick it from the list. If it doesn't show up, choose Enter it yourself.

Report a review or block someone

On a café page, tap ••• next to the review. Report as offensive sends it to us; we look at every report within 24 hours. Block hides all reviews by that person from you straight away.

Delete your account

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. Your account and all your reviews are removed for good.

Location

The app uses your location only to show cafés near you. You can turn it off any time in the iPhone Settings under Coffee List → Location.
